Lake Powell: The Perfect Staycation

Lake Powell is a large reservoir shaped like a winding, twisty river in southern Utah and northern Arizona. It is famous as a gorgeous and fun outdoor vacation spot, especially on a houseboat. You can do many things there, from water sports, to taking in the beautiful view from a houseboat, to hiking the nearby trails. You might even want to look for Skinfin, the legendary Lake Powell monster.

  1. The lovely scenery: the rocks are great!
  2. Swimming: the water is cool but not cold, so refreshing in summer heat.
  3. Boating: Plenty of room and the waters are calm.
  4. Jetskis: You can get to super high speeds and even pull someone behind you!
  5. Fishing: Even the inexperienced can catch fish here (live bait works best).
  6. Hiking: the amazing scenery is traversable; trails have different levels of difficulty.

I went to Lake Powell with my Young Men’s group and leaders. We fished, swam, and cooked, but my favorite thing to do was to ride an inflatable couch behind a jetski. It was super fun! So exhilarating!
